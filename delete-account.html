Account & Data Deletion

Lista+ (com.jorres.listaplus)

Last updated 26 August 2026

This page explains how to delete your Lista+ cloud account and the data stored with it, what gets removed, and what stays.

Important distinction. Lista+ keeps your ledger on your phone and works without an account. Deleting your cloud account removes the backup copy on our servers. It does not delete the ledger on your phone — your records stay exactly where they are and the app keeps working offline.

Option 1 — Delete it yourself in the app

This is immediate and requires no waiting.

  1. Open Lista+.
  2. Go to the More tab.
  3. Find the Account section (visible when you are signed in).
  4. Tap Delete cloud account.
  5. Confirm by tapping Delete forever.

Your account and its backed-up data are erased right away, and you are signed out on this device. Any other device still signed in to that account is signed out automatically the next time it tries to sync.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

Use this if you can no longer open the app, have lost the device, or cannot sign in.

Email devteam0309@gmail.com from the same Google address you used to sign in, with the subject "Delete my Lista+ account".

We use the sending address to confirm the request is yours, so a request from a different address cannot be honoured. Requests are completed within 30 days and normally much sooner.

What is deleted

Deleted permanently from our servers
Your account record, including the email address and Google account identifier used to sign in
All backed-up customers — names, nicknames, phone numbers, addresses, credit limits and notes
All backed-up products
All backed-up credit transactions and their line items
All backed-up payments and their allocations
Your cloud Premium status

Deletion is immediate and permanent. We do not keep an archived copy, and deleted data cannot be restored afterwards — by you or by us.

What is not deleted

  • The ledger on your phone. It remains fully intact and usable offline. To remove it as well, see below.
  • A Premium purchase made through Google Play. You paid for that and it is not part of what you asked to delete, so it survives and can be restored. Only Premium granted through your cloud account is removed.
  • Records of a purchase held by Google Play, which Google keeps under its own policies and we do not control.
To delete the ledger on your phone as well, uninstall Lista+ (or use Android Settings → Apps → Lista+ → Storage → Clear data). This is irreversible: if you have not backed up, those records cannot be recovered by anyone, including us. Delete your cloud account first if you want both gone.

Deleting individual customers instead

If you only want to remove one person's information rather than everything, you do not need to delete your account. Open the customer in the app and delete them — this removes their details along with their credit and payment history, on your phone and from the backup. This is the route to use when a customer asks you to remove their information.
