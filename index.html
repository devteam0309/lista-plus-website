Privacy Policy

Effective 26 August 2026 · Last updated 26 August 2026

The short version. Lista+ works entirely offline and keeps your ledger on your own phone. Nothing is uploaded unless you sign in and turn on cloud backup. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and your data is never sold or shared with anyone for their own purposes.

1. Two kinds of people in this app

  • You, the shop owner. You install and use Lista+, and you may create a cloud account.
  • Your customers. They never installed the app. You record details about them so you can remember who owes what.
Your responsibility as the person entering that information. When you record a customer's name, phone number, address or notes, you are handling another person's personal information. Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) you decide what to collect, so you are responsible for telling your customers that you keep this record, using it only for your ledger, and correcting or removing it when they ask. Lista+ gives you the tools; it cannot obtain their consent for you.

3. What leaves your phone, and when

Nothing leaves your phone until you sign in with Google and cloud backup is active. If you never sign in, Lista+ makes no network requests carrying your data and this section does not apply to you.

If you do enable cloud backup, these are copied to our server so they can be restored or used on another device: customers (including names, phone numbers, addresses and notes), products, credit transactions and their line items, and payments and their allocations. We also store the email address and account identifier Google provides at sign-in, plus whether your account has Premium.

Your local owner password is never uploaded — not even as a hash.

4. What Lista+ does not do

  • No analytics or tracking of any kind. No analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or attribution software.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier is collected.
  • We never sell your data, and never share it with third parties for their own purposes.
  • No access to contacts, messages, call log, camera, microphone, files or location. The app requests only two permissions: internet and network status. Tapping to call or text a customer opens Android's own app with the number filled in.
  • Biometrics never leave your device. Android performs the check; Lista+ only receives a yes or no.

6. Security

  • All network traffic uses HTTPS.
  • Your owner password is stored only as a salted PBKDF2 hash.
  • Sign-in tokens and Premium status are kept in Android's encrypted storage.
  • Optional app lock uses your fingerprint, face or screen lock.
  • Credentials are excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup.

No system is perfectly secure. If your phone is lost or shared, anyone who can unlock it may be able to open your ledger — enabling app lock is the best protection available in the app.

7. How long data is kept

Data on your phone stays until you delete it or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes the local ledger permanently. Backed-up data is kept until you delete your cloud account.

8. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 you may ask to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, object to its processing, or obtain a copy. The in-app tools let you do most of this yourself. If you believe your rights have been violated, you may complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

Deleting Your Account & Data

Last updated 26 August 2026

Important distinction. Lista+ keeps your ledger on your phone and works without an account. Deleting your cloud account removes the backup copy on our servers. It does not delete the ledger on your phone — your records stay exactly where they are and the app keeps working offline.

Option 1 — Delete it yourself in the app

Immediate, with no waiting.

  1. Open Lista+
  2. Go to the More tab
  3. Find the Account section (visible when you are signed in)
  4. Tap Delete cloud account
  5. Confirm with Delete forever

Your account and backed-up data are erased right away and you are signed out. Any other device still signed in is signed out the next time it tries to sync.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

Use this if you can no longer open the app, lost the device, or cannot sign in.

Email devteam0309@gmail.com from the same Google address you used to sign in, with the subject "Delete my Lista+ account". We use the sending address to confirm the request is yours, so a request from a different address cannot be honoured. Requests are completed within 30 days, and normally much sooner.

What is deleted

  • Your account record, including the email address and Google account identifier
  • All backed-up customers — names, nicknames, phone numbers, addresses, credit limits and notes
  • All backed-up products
  • All backed-up credit transactions and their line items
  • All backed-up payments and their allocations
  • Your cloud Premium status

Deletion is immediate and permanent. We keep no archived copy, and deleted data cannot be restored afterwards — by you or by us.

What is not deleted

  • The ledger on your phone. It remains fully intact and usable offline.
  • A Premium purchase made through Google Play. You paid for that and it is not part of what you asked to delete, so it survives and can be restored.
  • Records of a purchase held by Google Play, which Google keeps under its own policies.
To delete the ledger on your phone as well, uninstall Lista+ (or use Android Settings → Apps → Lista+ → Storage → Clear data). This is irreversible: if you have not backed up, those records cannot be recovered by anyone, including us. Delete your cloud account first if you want both gone.

Removing one customer instead

If you only want to remove one person's information, you do not need to delete your account. Open that customer in the app and delete them — this removes their details along with their credit and payment history, on your phone and from the backup. This is the route to use when a customer asks you to remove their information.
