Privacy Policy

Lista+ (com.jorres.listaplus) — a utang ledger for small shops

Effective 26 August 2026 · Last updated 26 August 2026

Lista+ helps a shop owner keep a record of credit (utang) extended to their customers. This policy explains what the app stores, what leaves your phone, and what never does.

The short version. Lista+ works entirely offline and keeps your ledger on your own phone. Nothing is uploaded unless you sign in and turn on cloud backup. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and your data is never sold or shared with anyone for their own purposes.

1. Two kinds of people in this app

This matters more here than in most apps, so it comes first.

  • You, the shop owner. You install and use Lista+. You may create a cloud account.
  • Your customers. They do not use the app and never installed it. You record details about them so you can remember who owes what.
Your responsibility as the person entering that information. When you record a customer's name, phone number, address, or notes, you are handling another person's personal information. Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), you are the one who decides what to collect, so you are responsible for telling your customers that you keep this record, using it only for your ledger, and correcting or removing it when they ask. Lista+ gives you the tools; it cannot obtain their consent for you. A good practice is simply telling people, "I'll save your name and number in my ledger app so I can track your utang."

Everything below lives in a database on your device and is the authoritative copy. The app is offline-first: it works with no internet connection at all.

3. What leaves your phone, and when

Nothing leaves your phone until you sign in with Google and cloud backup is active. If you never sign in, Lista+ makes no network requests carrying your data and this entire section does not apply to you.

If you do enable cloud backup

These records are copied to our server so they can be restored or used on another device:

  • Customers — including their names, phone numbers, addresses and notes
  • Products
  • Credit transactions and their line items
  • Payments and their allocations

We also store the email address and account identifier that Google provides when you sign in, so we know which ledger is yours, plus whether your account has Premium.

Your local owner password is never uploaded — not even as a hash. It stays on the device.

4. What Lista+ does not do

  • No analytics or tracking of any kind. The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or attribution software.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier is collected.
  • We never sell your data, and never share it with third parties for their own purposes.
  • No access to your phone's contacts, messages, call log, camera, microphone, files, or location. The app requests only two permissions: internet access and network-status. When you tap to call or text a customer, Android's own phone or messaging app opens with the number filled in — Lista+ never reads your contacts and never sends anything itself.
  • Biometrics never leave your device. If you enable app lock, Android performs the check; Lista+ only receives a yes or no and never sees your fingerprint or face data.

6. Security

  • All network traffic uses HTTPS.
  • Your owner password is stored only as a salted PBKDF2 hash.
  • Sign-in tokens and Premium status are kept in Android's encrypted storage.
  • Optional app lock uses your device fingerprint, face or screen lock.
  • Credentials are excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup so they do not leak into unrelated storage.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If your phone is lost or shared, anyone who can unlock it may be able to open your ledger — enabling app lock is the best protection available in the app.

7. How long data is kept

Data on your phone stays until you delete it or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes the local ledger permanently; if you have not enabled cloud backup, that copy cannot be recovered by anyone, including us.

Backed-up data is kept until you delete your cloud account. See Account & Data Deletion for how to do that and what happens.

8. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 you may ask to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, object to its processing, or obtain a copy. The in-app tools let you do most of this yourself: edit or delete any customer at any time, and delete your cloud account outright. For anything else, contact us below.

If you believe your rights have been violated, you may complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

9. Children

Lista+ is a business record-keeping tool intended for shop owners and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
